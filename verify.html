Age verification

Icebreaker Chat is for adults only. Everyone confirms they are 18 or older before they can chat or withdraw.

What you need

  • One valid photo ID: [list the documents your provider accepts in each country, e.g. national ID, passport, driving licence].
  • A phone or computer with a camera, and good light.
  • About 2 minutes.

What happens

  1. You photograph the front (and back, if asked) of your ID.
  2. You take a short selfie so we can check the ID belongs to you.
  3. Our verification provider, [provider name], checks the ID and works out your age.
  4. You get the result by email, usually within [time]. Some checks are reviewed by a person and take longer.

Your information

Your ID and selfie are used only to confirm your age. What we keep, who holds it and for how long is explained in the privacy policy. Other members never see your ID or selfie, and the check does not change the display name they see.

You are verified

Thank you. Your age is confirmed, so you can now chat and request withdrawals.

We are reviewing your check

A member of our team needs to look at your documents. This usually takes [time], and we will email you the result. You can't chat or withdraw until it is done.

We couldn't verify your age

This can happen if a photo was blurry, the ID was expired, or the name or date of birth did not match. Try again with a clear photo and good light. If you are under 18, you can't use Icebreaker Chat.
