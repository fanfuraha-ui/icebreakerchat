What it's like

You pick the hours. There are no shifts and no targets. Chat for ten minutes on the matatu home, or for an hour after dinner.

You choose how you're paid. Request a payout to M-Pesa, MoMo, a bank account or PayPal.

Your details stay yours. Other members see your display name. They never see your real name, phone number or email.

Someone is there if it goes wrong. Contact the support team and they will look into it.

The rules

Four rules, for every member and every chat.

  • Adults only, checked. Everyone is 18 or older, confirmed with an ID and selfie before chatting or withdrawing.
  • Be yourself. You chat as you, under your own display name.
  • No money between members. Never send or accept payments, gifts or contact details in a chat.
  • Report problems. Tell support about any conversation that makes you uncomfortable, and it will be reviewed.

Questions

How much can I earn?

It depends on how many conversations you complete. The current rate is shown in your dashboard before you start, so you always know what a chat is worth.

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Creating an account is free, and you will never be asked to pay to unlock earnings or withdrawals.

Do I need experience or special equipment?

No. You need a phone or computer with internet access.

How do withdrawals work?

Once your balance reaches the minimum shown in your dashboard, request a withdrawal to M-Pesa, MoMo, a bank account or PayPal. Processing times are listed in the terms.

Who will I be talking to?

Adult members who signed up to have conversations. You always chat as yourself, using your display name.

Is my personal information safe?

Other members see only your display name, not your real name, phone number or email. You can ask support to close your account and delete your data, as explained in the privacy policy.

Why do I have to verify my age?

Members talk with adults they have never met, so everyone must be 18 or older. After you sign up, you confirm your age with a photo of your ID and a selfie. You can't chat or withdraw until the check is done. See how verification works.

What happens to my ID and selfie?

They are used only to confirm your age. What is kept, by whom and for how long is set out in the privacy policy, and you can ask support about your data at any time.

Can I do this alongside work or school?

Yes. There are no shifts or targets, so you can chat whenever you have free time.

How do I know Icebreaker Chat is legitimate?

Read the terms and privacy policy, check that you are never asked for a joining fee, and contact support with any question before you start.
